To redact text in a PDF on a laptop offline, open the file in an editor that runs on your own computer, mark the words with a real Redact tool and save. In Gold PDF you press **R**, drag over the text and press Ctrl+S: each redacted page is rebuilt without the original words, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Quick steps
1. Open the PDF in Gold PDF (Ctrl+O, or drag the file onto the window). You can switch Wi-Fi off first if you want to be sure.
2. Use **Save as** (Ctrl+Shift+S) straight away so the original stays untouched.
3. Press Ctrl+F and search for each name, number or phrase you want to hide, so you know where every occurrence is.
4. Press **R** and drag a box over each one, slightly larger than the text.
5. Press Ctrl+S. The pages with redactions are rebuilt.
6. Close the file, reopen the saved copy, search again and try copying across each bar into Notepad. Nothing should come back.

## Why redact offline on a laptop
A laptop goes everywhere: trains, cafes, airport lounges, client offices. Those are the places where Wi-Fi is slow, shared or unknown, and also the places where people are most likely to be in a hurry to send a document. Redacting offline solves two separate problems at once.

The first is privacy. If the editor is a website, your unredacted document travels to someone else's server before it is redacted. That is the wrong order: the whole point is to stop the sensitive words leaving your hands. An offline editor reads the file from your disk, changes it on your disk and writes it back to your disk. The unredacted copy never moves. If you want the longer argument, see [is an online PDF editor safe for confidential documents](/articles/is-online-pdf-editor-safe-for-confidential-documents/).

The second is practicality. You do not need a connection to finish the job. You can redact on a plane, in a waiting room or on a tethered phone connection you do not want to spend data on. When you reach a network you only have to attach the already-clean file to an email.

## What "offline" really means
Offline redaction means every step that touches the sensitive content happens on the laptop itself: opening, searching, marking, rebuilding and saving. It does not mean the laptop must stay disconnected forever, only that the work does not depend on a connection.

Two things are worth checking in any tool you try.

- **Does it need to sign in?** Some programs run locally but still demand an account or a periodic check-in before they will let you save. Gold PDF needs no account.
- **Does anything leave the machine?** Gold PDF's redaction, editing and text recognition all run locally. The only feature that contacts the internet is the optional Gold AI panel, which sends text to the provider you choose with your own key, and only when you press Run. If you never open it, nothing is sent. For a very sensitive file, redact first and leave AI alone.

## How to redact text in a PDF on a laptop offline, step by step
### Find everything before you mark anything
Searching first stops the most common failure, which is hiding a name on page one and leaving it in a footer on page nine. Press Ctrl+F and try the full name, the surname alone, initials, an email address and the last four digits of any number. Write the hits down or note the pages. Search will not see text that is part of a picture, so scroll through the pages by eye as well.

### Mark the text with the Redact tool
Press **R** and drag a rectangle over each item. Make the box a little larger than the words so no tails of letters peek out at the edges. If you cover too much, Ctrl+Z undoes the last mark. You can mark as many areas as you like across as many pages as you like before saving.

### Save so the pages are rebuilt
Nothing is removed until you save. When you press Ctrl+S, Gold PDF rebuilds every page that contains a redaction as a sharp image with the black bars burned in, adds the remaining words back as an invisible text layer so the page stays searchable, removes orphaned objects and clears the document title and author. Pages without redactions stay exactly as they were, including live form fields.

### Test the saved file as the recipient would
Close the document and open the saved copy. Search for every term you redacted. Drag across a bar, copy and paste into Notepad. Click a bar and try to move it. Zoom in on the edges. Open the document properties. The checks take a couple of minutes and are laid out in [how to check if a PDF redaction is secure](/articles/how-to-check-if-a-pdf-redaction-is-secure/).

## Offline redaction on scanned pages
A scan is a photograph of a page, so it has no words to search. Run **Recognize text** (Ctrl+Shift+O) first. English and Hebrew recognition models are bundled with the program, so this step also works with no connection. Once the page has a text layer you can search it, find the words and mark them. When you save, the page is rebuilt, which also removes the covered pixels from the scan. There is a fuller walk-through in [how to redact a scanned PDF document](/articles/how-to-redact-a-scanned-pdf-document/).

Recognition is not perfect on faint, skewed or handwritten pages. If a word is missed by search, you can still drag a Redact box over it by eye, because redaction works on the area you mark and not only on recognised words.

## Battery, speed and file size on a laptop
Redaction is light work, so it will not drain the battery. Rebuilding runs only on pages that contain redactions, so redacting two pages of a fifty-page contract is quick. The trade-off is that a rebuilt page is stored as an image plus a text layer, so the file can become larger than the original. If you plan to email it, check the size before sending and consider redacting only the pages that need it.

## Habits that keep an offline job safe
- **Work on a copy.** Keep the unredacted original in a separate folder with a clear name, and send only the redacted file.
- **Name the output neutrally.** A file called "smith-salary-unredacted-v2.pdf" leaks before anyone opens it.
- **Mind the other copies.** Cloud-synced folders, email drafts and the Downloads folder may already hold copies of the original. Offline redaction cannot reach those.
- **Lock the laptop.** Redacting on a train is fine, but close the file when you stand up.
- **Password protect if you email it.** See [how to password protect a PDF for free](/articles/how-to-password-protect-a-pdf-for-free/) for the steps.

> **Common mistakes**
> - Drawing a black rectangle, highlighter or Whiteout and calling it redaction.
> - Redacting the first occurrence of a name and missing the rest.
> - Testing the copy that is still open instead of reopening the saved file.
> - Forgetting that the original is still sitting in your Downloads folder or an email draft.
> - Running text recognition on a poor scan and trusting that search found everything.

## What Gold PDF does and does not do
Gold PDF is a free, offline PDF editor for Windows 10 and 11. Its Redact tool (**R**) rebuilds each redacted page on save, so the covered text, images and drawings are replaced and not just hidden, and it clears the title and author at the same time. Built-in text recognition (Ctrl+Shift+O, English and Hebrew bundled) lets you search scans without a connection, and it needs no account. It does not run on Mac, Linux, phones or in a browser, it does not produce court-specific redaction logs, and it cannot decide what is sensitive for you: you still have to search, mark every occurrence and test the result. Its plain Whiteout tool only covers content, so use Redact, not Whiteout, for anything confidential. The tool itself is explained on the [redact PDF](/redact-pdf/) page.
