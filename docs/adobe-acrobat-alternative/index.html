A free Adobe Acrobat alternative for Windows

Gold PDF covers what most people use Acrobat Pro for — editing text, redaction, OCR, forms, signatures, passwords and page tools — and it is free, offline and needs no account. Acrobat Pro costs about $19.99 a month on an annual plan (list price, October 2026; check Adobe).

How to do it in 3 steps

  1. Install. Run the installer — no admin rights needed.
  2. Open your PDFs. Double-click any PDF.
  3. Work. Use the same tasks: edit, redact, OCR, sign, protect.

Why Gold PDF

  • $0 instead of a yearly subscription.
  • Private — files stay on your PC.
  • 30 interface languages, right-to-left layouts included.
  • Honest limits — Windows only, no Mac yet, installer unsigned.

Questions

What does Gold PDF not do?

It is not a prepress or accessibility-tagging suite, has no Mac version and no cloud signing service.
