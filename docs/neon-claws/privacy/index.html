Privacy Policy

Cat Fighting Games: 2 Player (also called Neon Claws: The Ninth Life Tournament) · Android app, package com.dannanahman.neonclaws · Last updated: 30 October 2026

This game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. This page explains what that means in detail.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or device identifiers.
  • It does not show advertisements and does not use advertising IDs.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools, and it does not send any data to us or to third parties.
  • It does not need an internet connection to play.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, level, unlocked cats and skins, trophies, settings such as language and volume, and your daily reward streak) is saved only in the app's local storage on your own device. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted.

Permissions

The app may use the vibration feature of your phone for game feedback if you leave vibration turned on in Settings. It requests no other sensitive permissions.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. It contains cartoon fighting between cats without blood.

Third-party material

The game contains music, sound effects and artwork by other creators, used under their open licenses and credited inside the game. These files are packaged inside the app and are not loaded from the internet.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting any data (for example, if ads or online features are added), this page will be updated and the Google Play listing will be changed to match before that version is released.
