Best Free PDF Editor With OCR

Updated 2026-10-18 · 2 min read · by Gold PDF

A good free PDF editor with OCR should recognise text locally, support your language, and let you edit the result. Gold PDF does: press Ctrl+Shift+O, English and Hebrew work offline, other languages download once, and you can then search, edit and redact the text.

In this guide
  1. What to check
  2. Gold PDF's approach

What to check

  • Offline or upload? Local OCR keeps documents private.
  • Languages. Does it include yours, including right-to-left scripts?
  • Output. Searchable PDF with the original look, or lower quality re-creation?
  • Editing. Can you fix lines after recognition?
  • Limits. Page caps or watermarks on the free plan?

Gold PDF's approach

OCR adds an invisible text layer over the original image so the page looks identical. You can search the results in a left-hand list, edit a line, or redact sensitive text. See how to OCR a scanned PDF for free.

Accuracy tips

Use 300 dpi, straight pages and clear contrast. Check numbers. Handwriting is unreliable for any OCR.

Common mistakes

- Judging accuracy from a blurry phone photo.- Not verifying digits in invoices.- Uploading sensitive scans to unknown sites.
