How to Redact Email Addresses From a PDF

Updated 2026-11-04 · 6 min read · by Gold PDF

To redact email addresses from a PDF, search the document for "@", mark every address with a Redact tool that removes the content underneath, and save. In Gold PDF, press Ctrl+F to find each address, press R to cover it, and Ctrl+S rebuilds the redacted pages so the original text is gone.

Quick steps

  1. Open the PDF in Gold PDF and choose Save as so the original stays untouched.
  2. Press Ctrl+F and search for @. Note how many results there are and which pages they are on.
  3. Search for common domains too (for example the company name or "mail"), because some addresses are written with spaces or as "name at domain".
  4. Press R and drag a box over each address. Make the box slightly wider than the text.
  5. Press Ctrl+S to save. Pages with redactions are rebuilt.
  6. Reopen the saved file, search for "@" again, and paste around each black bar into Notepad. Nothing should come back.

Why email addresses deserve special care

An email address looks harmless, but it is personal data in most privacy laws, and it connects a person to an account, a workplace or a case. Publishing a list of addresses invites spam and phishing, and in a court, HR or customer-service file it can identify someone who was meant to stay anonymous.

Email addresses also hide in more places than people expect. Beyond the main text you may find them in:

  • headers and footers repeated on every page
  • signature blocks at the end of letters
  • "cc" and "to" lines in a printed email thread
  • hyperlinks, where the visible text says "contact us" but the link target is a mailto address
  • form fields that hold a typed address
  • document properties such as the author or subject

A careful redaction covers all of these, not only the one you noticed first.

How to find every email address in a PDF

Searching is the fastest way to be thorough. In Gold PDF, press Ctrl+F and try these queries one after another:

Search forWhat it finds
@Almost every normally written address
.com, .org, .co.uk, .netAddresses whose "@" was split by a line break
the company or domain nameEvery address at that organisation
" at " and "[at]"Addresses written to avoid spam robots
mailtoText copied from link targets

Step through the results with the Enter key and place a redaction box as you go. Write down the page numbers; you will use the list again when you verify. If the same address appears on forty pages, the approach in how to redact multiple pages in a PDF at once saves a lot of time.

Redacting the addresses step by step

With the file open, press R. Drag a rectangle over the first address. If the box is too small and a letter peeks out, press Ctrl+Z and draw it again a little larger. When you press Ctrl+S, Gold PDF rebuilds each page that has a redaction as a sharp image with the black bars burned in, keeps the remaining words as an invisible text layer so the page is still searchable, and clears the title and author. Pages with no redactions stay exactly as they were.

You may not need to hide the whole address. Sometimes the right choice is to hide only the part before the "@" and keep the domain, for example when you want to show that a message came from a company without identifying the sender. Decide this before you start, and apply the same rule to every address so the document looks consistent.

A worked example: an email thread printed to PDF

Suppose you saved a three-page email conversation as a PDF to send to a landlord, and you want to remove the addresses of everyone except yourself. Open "thread.pdf" and use Save as to create "thread-redacted.pdf". Search for "@": there are eleven hits. Four are in the header lines of the messages, five are in the signature blocks, and two are inside the quoted text of an earlier message. Press R and cover all eleven, leaving your own address visible on purpose. Save, reopen, and search for "@" again. Only your own address is found. Select across each black bar and paste into Notepad: the clipboard stays empty. Finally search for the other people's domain names; they no longer appear. The file is ready to send, and for extra protection you can add a password using how to password protect a PDF for free.

Email addresses in links and metadata

A hyperlink has two parts: the text you see and the target it points to. If the text is "Contact the clerk" but the target is a mailto address, covering the words does not necessarily describe the target. This is why the saved file must be tested instead of trusted. Because Gold PDF rebuilds redacted pages, the link area under a redaction box is not kept, but links on pages you did not redact stay as they were. If an address might sit behind a link on a page with no visible address, check that page by hovering over its links.

Metadata is the other hiding place. Author and subject fields sometimes hold a name or an address. Redacted saves clear the title and author, and you can read more about the rest in how to remove metadata from a PDF before sharing.

Scanned PDFs and screenshots of emails

If the PDF is a scan or a screenshot, there is no text to search. Run Recognize text with Ctrl+Shift+O (English and Hebrew are bundled and work offline), then search for "@". Recognition is not perfect on small or blurry type, so also scan each page by eye. The rebuild on save replaces the covered pixels, so the picture of the address is not left behind. The full method is in how to redact a scanned PDF document.

Checking your work

Never judge by the screen alone. On the saved copy:

  1. Search for "@", for each redacted domain, and for each person's name.
  2. Select across every black bar, copy, and paste into a plain text editor.
  3. Hover over links on pages near the redactions.
  4. Open the document properties and confirm nothing identifying remains.
  5. Zoom to 100% and read each page once for addresses you missed.

If anything survives, return to the original, add the missing box, and save a fresh copy.

What Gold PDF does and does not do

Gold PDF is a free Windows application that works offline, requires no account and does not upload your documents. Its Redact tool removes the covered content from the pages it rebuilds. It does not find email addresses by itself: you search and choose what to cover, then verify the result. It is available for Windows, and the plain Whiteout tool only covers content and should not be used as a replacement for redaction. The reasons are explained in does drawing a black box redact a PDF.

Common mistakes

- Covering the address in the body but missing the same address in the header, footer or signature block.- Using a rectangle or whiteout and calling it redaction.- Overwriting the original instead of using Save as.- Skipping the final search for "@" on the saved file.

Frequently asked questions

How do I redact email addresses from a PDF?

Open the PDF in Gold PDF, search for the "@" sign to find every address, press R, drag over each one and save. The pages are rebuilt without the original text.

Can I find all email addresses at once?

Press Ctrl+F and search for "@" or for the domain, for example "gmail.com". Step through the results and mark each one, including addresses in headers, footers and links.

Does redaction remove the clickable mailto link?

Redacted pages are rebuilt, so the covered text and the area under it are not kept. Always click or search the saved file to confirm no address survives.

Are redacted email addresses still searchable?

They should not be. After saving, search the saved file for "@" and for each domain; only addresses you chose to keep should appear.
