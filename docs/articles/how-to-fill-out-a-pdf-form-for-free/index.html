How to Fill Out a PDF Form for Free

Updated 2026-10-18 · 2 min read · by Gold PDF

To fill out a PDF form for free, open it in Gold PDF. Fillable fields are highlighted automatically — click each one and type, tick boxes, choose from lists, add your signature and press Ctrl+S.

In this guide
  1. Quick steps
  2. Non-fillable forms
  3. Different languages
  4. Keep your data safe

Quick steps

  1. Open the form (Ctrl+O).
  2. Click a field and type; Tab moves on. Tick boxes and choose drop-down values.
  3. Place your saved signature (Signatures button).
  4. Save with Ctrl+S.

Non-fillable forms

Some PDFs are just pictures of forms. Press T for the Text tool and click where text belongs; set the size in the ribbon. For checkmarks, type an "X" or use the Pen. For scans, run Recognize text first if you also need to search.

Different languages

Type in Hebrew, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Thai and more — Gold PDF uses Windows fonts for the right script.

Keep your data safe

Forms contain personal data. Fill them offline, save to a safe place and password protect the file before emailing: how to password protect a PDF for free.

Common mistakes

- Printing to paper to fill a form that is already fillable.- Forgetting to save before closing.- Emailing a filled form without protection.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know a PDF is fillable?

Gold PDF highlights the fields softly in blue when you open the file.

What if the form is not fillable?

Use the Text tool (T) to type anywhere on the page.

Are my entries saved in the file?

Yes. Values are saved as real form data when you save.
