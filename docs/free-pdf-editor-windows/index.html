The free PDF editor for Windows with no watermark

Gold PDF is a free PDF editor for Windows 10 and 11. It edits text, redacts for real, recognises scanned text, fills forms, signs, merges pages and adds passwords — offline, with no account, no watermark and no time limit.

How to do it in 3 steps

  1. Download. Get the installer (about 118 MB).
  2. Install. Per-user install; double-click a PDF to open it.
  3. Edit. Use the ribbon or press Ctrl+K to search any command.

Why Gold PDF

  • No watermark, ever.
  • Tabs, thumbnails, search, undo/redo.
  • Optional AI with your own key.
  • 30 languages.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, everything except the optional AI feature.

Is there a catch?

No account, no trial, no watermark. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may warn once.
