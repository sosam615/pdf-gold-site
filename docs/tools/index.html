Free PDF tools

Twelve free PDF tools that run inside your browser: your files are never uploaded, there is no watermark and no sign-up. Pick a tool, drop a file, download the result.

  • Merge PDFCombine several PDFs into one document in your browser. Free, no sign-up, no watermark, and your files never leave your device.
  • Split PDFSplit a PDF by page ranges or into single pages, right in your browser. No upload, no watermark, no account.
  • Extract pagesPull selected pages out of a PDF into a new, smaller file. Free, private and instant — runs in your browser.
  • Delete pagesRemove blank, duplicate or unwanted pages from a PDF in seconds. Free, no watermark, and the file stays on your device.
  • Rotate PDFRotate all or selected pages of a PDF by 90, 180 or 270 degrees and save the fixed file. No upload, no watermark.
  • Compress PDFMake a large PDF smaller for email and forms. Free, runs in your browser, your document is never uploaded.
  • PDF to JPGTurn every page of a PDF into a high-quality JPG or PNG. Free, no upload, no watermark.
  • JPG to PDFTurn photos and screenshots into one PDF, in your order. Free, private, no watermark.
  • Page numbersNumber the pages of any PDF: choose position, starting number and format. No upload, no watermark.
  • Watermark PDFStamp DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or your own text across every page of a PDF. No upload, no sign-up.
  • PDF to textPull all selectable text out of a PDF and copy or download it as a .txt file. Free, private, runs in your browser.
  • Protect PDFAdd a password to a PDF so only people with the password can open it. Strong encryption, no upload, free.
  • Unlock PDFRemove the open-password from your own PDF so it opens without asking. Free, private, runs in your browser.
