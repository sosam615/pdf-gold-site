Split a PDF into separate files — free and private

To split a PDF, drop it here, type the page ranges you want (for example 1-3, 4-6, 7-) or choose one file per page, then press Split PDF. Each range becomes its own PDF, created inside your browser without uploading anything.

Drop your PDF hereor click to choose · stays on your device

    How to do it in 3 steps

    1. Add the PDF. Drag it in or click the box.
    2. Choose how to split. Type ranges such as 1-3, 4-6, 7- or pick one file per page.
    3. Download the parts. Save each file, or use Download all.

    About this tool

    Splitting is useful when a long scan contains several documents, when you need to send only a few pages, or when a form must be uploaded one page at a time. Ranges are separated by commas; an open range like 7- means "page 7 to the end".

    Every part keeps its original quality. If you only want to keep certain pages in a single file, use Extract Pages instead; to remove pages, use Delete PDF Pages.

    Why use it

    • Private: the file never leaves your device.
    • Flexible ranges, or every page separately.
    • No watermark and no sign-up.
    • Original text and images are preserved.

    Questions

    How do I split a PDF into single pages?

    Choose "Every page as its own file" and press Split PDF. You get one PDF per page.

    What does a range like 5- mean?

    It means page 5 through the last page of the document.

    Can I split a scanned PDF?

    Yes. Scanned pages are images and are split exactly like any other page.

    Is my document uploaded?

    No. Everything is processed in your browser.
