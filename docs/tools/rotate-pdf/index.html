Rotate PDF pages permanently — free and private

To rotate a PDF, drop it here, choose 90°, 180° or 270°, optionally enter which pages to rotate, and press Rotate PDF. The rotation is saved permanently in a new file, created inside your browser.

Drop your PDF hereor click to choose · stays on your device

    How to do it in 3 steps

    1. Add the PDF. Drag it in or click the box.
    2. Pick the angle. Rotate every page, or only the pages you list.
    3. Download. The new file opens the right way up everywhere.

    About this tool

    Scanners and phone cameras often produce sideways pages. Rotating in a viewer only changes what you see; this tool saves the new orientation in the file so it stays correct for everyone you send it to.

    Why use it

    • Rotation is saved in the file.
    • All pages or selected pages.
    • No upload, no watermark.
    • Text stays selectable.

    Questions

    Is the rotation permanent?

    Yes. The page rotation is written into the new PDF.

    Can I rotate only some pages?

    Yes. Enter page numbers like 2, 4-6; leave the box empty to rotate all.

    Does rotating lower quality?

    No. Pages are not re-rendered.

    Is my file uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser.
