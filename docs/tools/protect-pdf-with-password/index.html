Password-protect a PDF — encrypted in your browser

To password-protect a PDF, drop it here, type a password and press Protect PDF. The file is encrypted inside your browser, so neither the document nor your password is ever sent to a server.

Drop your PDF hereor click to choose · stays on your device

    How to do it in 3 steps

    1. Add the PDF. Drag it in or click the box.
    2. Choose a strong password. Use a long phrase you can remember.
    3. Download the protected PDF. It asks for the password every time it is opened.

    About this tool

    A password stops anyone without it from opening the document. Keep the password safe: it cannot be recovered. For documents with sensitive passages, combine this with real redaction in the free Gold PDF desktop editor, which also supports AES-256 passwords.

    Why use it

    • Encryption happens locally — password never leaves your device.
    • Works with any PDF viewer.
    • No watermark.
    • Free.

    Questions

    How strong is the encryption?

    It uses standard PDF encryption. For AES-256 and extra options, use the Gold PDF desktop app.

    Can I recover a forgotten password?

    No. Keep it somewhere safe.

    Is my password uploaded?

    No. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Can I remove the password later?

    Yes, with the Unlock PDF tool and the password.
