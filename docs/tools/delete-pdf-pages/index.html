Delete pages from a PDF — free, no upload

To delete pages from a PDF, drop the file here, enter the page numbers to remove (for example 3, 7-9) and press Delete pages. The cleaned PDF is created in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Drop your PDF hereor click to choose · stays on your device

    How to do it in 3 steps

    1. Add the PDF. Drag it in or click the box.
    2. List the pages to delete. Numbers and ranges: 3, 7-9.
    3. Download the result. The remaining pages keep their order.

    About this tool

    Typical reasons: removing a blank page from a scan, deleting a cover sheet, or dropping pages that contain information you should not share. Note that deleting a page here removes it from the new file; if you need to hide parts of a page instead, use real redaction in the free Gold PDF desktop editor.

    You cannot delete every page — at least one must remain.

    Why use it

    • Fast and private.
    • Remove single pages or whole ranges.
    • No watermark.
    • Works on any device with a modern browser.

    Questions

    How do I remove one page from a PDF?

    Type its number, for example 4, and press Delete pages.

    Can I undo it?

    Your original file is never changed, so just keep it and make a new version.

    Can I delete the last page?

    Yes — as long as at least one page remains.

    Will it reduce file size?

    Usually yes, because the deleted pages and their images are not copied.
