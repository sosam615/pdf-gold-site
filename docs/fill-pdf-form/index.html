How to fill out a PDF form for free

Open the form in Gold PDF. Fillable fields are highlighted automatically — click and type. Add a saved signature, then press Ctrl+S.

How to do it in 3 steps

  1. Open the form. Fields appear as soft blue boxes.
  2. Fill it in. Type, tick boxes and choose from drop-downs; Hebrew and other scripts work.
  3. Sign and save. Place a saved signature and save; the values are stored as real form data.

Why Gold PDF

  • No printing — fill and sign on screen.
  • Signature library — draw once, reuse forever.
  • Free and offline.

Questions

Can I fill a form that is not fillable?

Yes. Use the Text tool to type anywhere on the page.

Will the data be editable later?

Yes, saved fields stay live unless the page was rebuilt by a redaction.
